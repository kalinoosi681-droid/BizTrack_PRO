Margin Strategy Tips
When to Increase Margins:
  • High-demand luxury items (cosmetics, electronics)
  • Unique products with no local competition
  • Fast-moving convenience items
  • Seasonal or specialty products
When to Keep Margins Low:
  • Staple items (flour, bread, basic food)
  • Highly competitive products
  • High-volume, low-margin strategy items
  • Loss leaders to drive foot traffic
